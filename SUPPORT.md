## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I log water?**
Tap any of the quick-add buttons on the home screen, or tap the + button in the top right to enter a custom amount. You can choose between water, tea, coffee, juice, milk, sparkling, smoothie, or other.

**How do I set my daily goal?**
Go to Settings → Daily Goal and use the +/− buttons to adjust your goal in 250ml or 8oz increments.

**What units does the app support?**
Hidrate supports both metric (ml) and imperial (fl oz). Switch between them in Settings → Daily Goal → Unit toggle.

---

### Hydration Tracking

**Why does coffee count for less than water?**
Hidrate applies beverage hydration factors based on general wellness research. Coffee (0.8×), tea (0.9×), and other beverages contribute slightly less toward your goal than plain water. These are estimates for general wellness purposes and may vary by individual.

**Does the app sync with Apple Health?**
Yes. Enable HealthKit in Settings or during onboarding to sync your water intake with Apple Health.

**Does the app work with Apple Watch?**
Yes. Install the Hidrate Watch app to log water directly from your wrist. Data syncs automatically with your iPhone.

---

### Streaks & Achievements

**How do streaks work?**
You maintain your streak by meeting your daily hydration goal each day. Missing a day breaks your streak unless you use a Streak Freeze.

**Why didn't I get credit for yesterday?**
Entries are counted for the calendar day they are logged. Make sure you log before midnight to count toward that day's goal.

---

### Account & Social

**How do I add friends?**
Go to the Social tab and share your unique code with a friend, or enter their code to send a friend request.

**Is Sign in with Apple required?**
No. Social features (friends, leaderboards, nudges) require Sign in with Apple. Core hydration tracking works without an account.

**Where is my data stored?**
All data is stored locally on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account.

---


### Troubleshooting

**My data disappeared.**
If you signed in with Apple, your data syncs via iCloud. Check that iCloud is enabled in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Hidrate. You can also check Settings → Data inside the app.
