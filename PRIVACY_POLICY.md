# Hidrate Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 2026**

Hidrate ("we", "our", or "the app") is a hydration tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Display Name**: Optional name you provide for your profile and social features
- **Apple ID**: Used for Sign in with Apple authentication (we only receive a unique identifier, not your email unless you choose to share it)

### Health & Fitness Data
- **Water Intake**: Hydration logs you manually enter
- **HealthKit Data**: With your permission, we read and write water intake data to Apple Health

### Usage Data
- Hydration history, streaks, achievements, and app preferences stored locally and synced via iCloud

---

## How We Use Your Information

- To track and display your hydration progress
- To calculate personalized daily hydration goals
- To sync your data across your Apple devices via iCloud
- To enable social features (friend connections, leaderboards, nudges)
- To send hydration reminders (with your permission)

---

## Data Storage & Sync

All your data is stored:
- **Locally** on your device using SwiftData
- **In your personal iCloud account** via CloudKit (if signed in)

We do not operate external servers. Your data remains within the Apple ecosystem under your Apple ID.

---

## Social Features

If you use social features:
- Your display name, share code, hydration progress, streak, and level may be visible to friends you connect with
- Friend connections require mutual consent via share codes or friend requests
- You can remove friends at any time

---

## Third-Party Services

We use only Apple services:
- **Apple HealthKit** - Health data integration
- **Apple CloudKit** - Data sync and social features
- **Apple Push Notifications** - Reminders and friend notifications
- **Apple Sign In** - Authentication
- **Apple StoreKit** - In-app purchases

**We do not share your data with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data brokers.**

---

## In-App Purchases

Premium features are available via in-app purchase. Purchase history is managed by Apple and subject to Apple's privacy policy.

---

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time by:
- Deleting entries within the app
- Removing the app from your device
- Managing iCloud storage in iOS Settings

---

## Children's Privacy

Hidrate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
